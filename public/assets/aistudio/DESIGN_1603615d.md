## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a **Modern Minimalist Academic** aesthetic tailored for institutional clarity, transactional integrity, and effortless ticketing workflows. It balances the authoritative weight of an established university organization with the clean, airy, high-legibility ergonomics of modern productivity software.

### Brand Personality & Core Principles
- **Authoritative & Trustworthy:** Anchored by structured deep electric blue foundations, communicating legitimacy, financial transparency, and bureaucratic order without feeling sterile or antiquated.
- **Calm & Frictionless:** High proportions of cool slate whites and generous negative space eliminate panic during high-stress moments (such as ticket drops, checkout counters, and gate validation check-ins).
- **Legible Utility:** Critical verification metadata—such as validation codes, seat tiers, pricing, and student identification—prioritizes instantaneous scanability over decorative flourishes.

### Aesthetic Direction
A tailored fusion of **Minimalist Institutional Architecture** and **Refined Micro-Interactions**:
- Strict 1px hairline divider systems rather than heavy brutalist boundaries.
- Low-contrast, ambient ambient soft shadows that lift active elements gently above neutral slate backdrops.
- Purpose-driven color distribution: deep electric blue grounds the interface; vibrant royal blue guides actionable user journeys; bold crimson contextual semantic badges convey gate and payment states at a glance.

## Colors

The color architecture enforces a clean, institutional hierarchy with deliberate functional separation between brand elements, primary interaction pathways, tier accents, and event-gate status tokens.

### Palette Construction
- **Primary Canvas & Chrome:**
  - `Base Canvas`: `#f8f9ff` (Cool Slate Tint) creates a soft, glare-free background.
  - `Surface / Card Base`: `#ffffff` (Crisp White) provides contrast against slate layers.
  - `Surface Subdued`: `#eff4ff` for table headers, inactive segments, and structural backings.
  - `Hairline Border`: `#c6c6cd` for precise, low-contrast 1px separation.
- **Text & Brand Tone:**
  - `Header & Strong Label`: `#0b1c30` (Deep Academic Slate/Navy) provides authoritative contrast.
  - `Body Copy`: `#45464d` ensures maximum readability across diverse displays.
  - `Muted / Helper Text`: `#767683` for meta captions, secondary labels, and timestamps.
- **Interactive & Accent Accents:**
  - `Interactive Primary`: `#0a2cfd` (Vibrant Electric Blue) dedicated to primary triggers, active progress states, and selection boundaries.
  - `Tier Accent`: `#ae1400` (Bold Crimson) reserved for VIP tiers, early-bird callouts, and highlighted notifications.

### Semantic Status Architecture
All gate, payment, and validation tokens follow a 3-element paradigm (soft tinted background, subtle 1px border, high-contrast dark text):
- **Success / Lunas / Boleh Masuk:** Background `#ECFDF5`, Border `#10B981`, Text `#047857`.
- **Warning / Pending / Sudah Pernah Scan:** Background `#FFFBEB`, Border `#F59E0B`, Text `#B45309`.
- **Danger / Rejected / Tiket Tidak Valid:** Background `#FEF2F2`, Border `#EF4444`, Text `#B91C1C`.
- **Neutral / Sold Out / Expired:** Background `#F1F5F9`, Border `#CBD5E1`, Text `#475569`.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy relies on a tri-font structure that guarantees structural authority, effortless paragraph comprehension, and high-accuracy numerical verification.

### Font Allocations
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Headings):** Modern geometric sans with open apertures and clean terminals. Used exclusively for page titles, card headers, section names, and tier pricing announcements.
- **Inter (Body & Forms):** Workhorse sans engineered for screen rendering. Delivers neutral, highly legible copy across form fields, checkout instructions, modal bodies, and helper descriptions.
- **JetBrains Mono (Technical & Verification):** Strict tabular monospace used across data-sensitive contexts: ticket serial strings, QR transaction IDs, gate timestamps, seat designations, and monetary values (`Rp 75.000`).

### Usage Rules
- Numerical currency values must strictly pair `code-md` or `code-lg` with `font-weight: 600` to prevent baseline shifting in dynamic lists.
- Avoid using all-caps for strings exceeding 12 characters; restrict uppercase to monospace ticket prefixes (e.g., `HMK-2025-X01`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column fluid grid on desktop transitioning seamlessly into a single-column, touch-optimized structure on mobile viewports.

### Breakpoint Structure & Grid Parameters
- **Mobile (`320px` to `639px`):** 4 columns. Gutter: `1rem` (`16px`). Margin: `1rem` (`16px`). All interactive targets maintain at minimum `44px` physical touch bounds. Horizontal overflow is strictly barred (`overflow-x: hidden`).
- **Tablet (`640px` to `1023px`):** 8 columns. Gutter: `1.25rem` (`20px`). Margin: `1.5rem` (`24px`).
- **Desktop (`1024px+`):** 12 columns with max container width capped at `1200px` centered. Gutter: `1.5rem` (`24px`). Margin: `2rem` (`32px`).

### Reflow Principles
- **Transactions & Orders:** Shift from clean, high-density desktop data tables directly to structured summary cards on viewports under `768px`.
- **Checkout Bar:** On mobile, payment summary actions dock to the viewport base with an elevation layer (`sticky bottom-0`) containing `space-md` safe-area padding.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses flat, high-clarity structural layers accented by muted ambient shadows. This keeps the institutional layout sharp while preventing visual visual noise during critical review processes.

### Surface Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Ground color `#f8f9ff`. Zero elevation. Default for page canvas, full-width dividers, and backdrop panels.
- **Level 1 (Card & Section Surfaces):** Color `#ffffff`, bounded by `1px solid #c6c6cd`. Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(17, 48, 255, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(17, 48, 255, 0.02)`. Used for content cards, data tables, and input groupings.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Elements & Dropdowns):** Color `#ffffff`, bounded by `1px solid #c6c6cd`. Shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(17, 48, 255, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(17, 48, 255, 0.04)`. Applied to active popovers, filter menus, and hovered card states.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Verification Overlays):** Color `#ffffff`, bounded by `1px solid #c6c6cd`. Shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(17, 48, 255, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(17, 48, 255, 0.03)`. Backed by an institutional dimming backdrop: `rgba(11, 28, 48, 0.45)` with a `backdrop-blur(4px)`.

### Border Guidelines
Never deploy borders exceeding 1px width. Visual distinction must be created by tonal contrast between `#c6c6cd` and `#767683`, never by heavy stroke weights.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a balanced, approachable geometry with standard 8px (`0.5rem`) base radii, scaling up to 12px–16px for larger cards and modals. This avoids both harsh industrial edges and overly informal bubble shapes.

### Shape Hierarchy
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Badges):** `rounded` at `0.5rem` (`8px`).
- **Cards, Panels, and Containers:** `rounded-lg` at `0.75rem` (`12px`) or `rounded-xl` at `1rem` (`16px`).
- **Pill Accents:** Reserved strictly for numeric notification counters, live scanner reticle rings, and status chip capsules (`rounded-full`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#0b1c30` background, `#ffffff` text, `0.5rem` radius. Subtle hover state transition to `#45464d`. Focused state: 2px ring `#0a2cfd` with 2px white offset. Minimum height of `44px` on mobile screens.
- **Interactive Alternate:** Solid `#0a2cfd` background, `#ffffff` text. Used for primary checkout and proceed triggers.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background `#ffffff`, border `1px solid #c6c6cd`, text `#0b1c30`. Hover: background `#f8f9ff`.
- **Destructive:** Background `#FEF2F2`, border `1px solid #EF4444`, text `#B91C1C`.

### Status Badges & Chips
- Built with a strict pill or `0.375rem` shape.
- Height: `24px` to `28px`.
- Consists of: 6px solid circular status indicator, label in `JetBrains Mono` or `Inter SemiBold` (0.75rem).
- Always uses the 3-layer color token system:
  - **Lunas / Boleh Masuk:** `#ECFDF5` bg, `#10B981` border, `#047857` text.
  - **Pending / Pernah Scan:** `#FFFBEB` bg, `#F59E0B` border, `#B45309` text.
  - **Tidak Valid / Gagal:** `#FEF2F2` bg, `#EF4444` border, `#B91C1C` text.
  - **Expired / Habis:** `#F1F5F9` bg, `#CBD5E1` border, `#475569` text.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Standard height: `44px` for touch parity.
- Background: `#ffffff`. Border: `1px solid #c6c6cd`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#0a2cfd` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(10, 44, 253, 0.15)`).
- Error state: Border `#EF4444`, focus ring `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.15)`, error message below in `Inter Regular 0.75rem` (`#B91C1C`).
- Labels: `Plus Jakarta Sans Medium 0.875rem`, text `#0b1c30`.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Box dimensions: `18px × 18px` with 4px radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios.
- Unchecked: `1px solid #c6c6cd` on `#ffffff`.
- Checked: `#0a2cfd` fill with crisp `#ffffff` checkmark/dot icon.
- Hover: Border color deepens to `#0b1c30`.

### Cards & Content Containers
- Standard Card: `#ffffff` surface, `1px solid #c6c6cd`, `0.75rem` (`12px`) radius, Level 1 shadow. Inner padding: `space-md` (`16px`) on mobile, `space-lg` (`24px`) on desktop.
- Ticket Stub Card: Divided by a horizontal dashed hairline `#c6c6cd` with dual inward circular notches (radius 10px) mimicking a perforated physical ticket.

### Lists vs. Tables (Reflow Model)
- **Desktop (>768px):** Tabular data structure with fixed table header `#f8f9ff`, hairline horizontal dividers `#c6c6cd`, numeric alignment set to right with `JetBrains Mono`.
- **Mobile (<=768px):** Table converts into discrete stacked cards. Each card displays ticket ID, attendee name, and status badge at top, with pricing and validation triggers spanning full width at base.
